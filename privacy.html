RunGuard privacy notice

RunGuard helps keep Trackmania leaderboards honest. It is a hobby project made by one player. It is not made by Nadeo or Ubisoft, and I am not trying to collect anything about you beyond what is needed to check runs.

What is sent

When you finish a run that places in the world top 20 on its map, RunGuard sends a report to a review server. The report contains:

  • your Trackmania display name and login
  • the map and your finish time
  • driving statistics: top speed, peak acceleration, respawns, where you started, how fast you press keys
  • anything unusual the checks noticed
  • the names of the Openplanet plugins you have enabled, and whether Openplanet developer mode is on
  • the RunGuard settings you use

What is not sent

RunGuard does not send your files, passwords or anything outside Trackmania and Openplanet. Like any website, the server can see your IP address, but RunGuard does not store it.

Who can read it, and for how long

Only moderators I approve can read reports. Runs where nothing suspicious was found, or that a moderator has cleared, are deleted after 30 days. Runs confirmed as cheating are kept so the decision can be checked.

Your choices

Uploads are off until you agree in the plugin, and you can turn them off at any time in RunGuard's settings. To ask a question or to have your data deleted, contact YOUR-DISCORD-OR-EMAIL.
